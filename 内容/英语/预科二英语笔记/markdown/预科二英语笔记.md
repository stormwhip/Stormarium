## Active Learning Strategies

1. Ask yourself pre-reading questions.  
   向自己提出读前问题.

2. Identify and define any unfamiliar terms.  
   找出并解释任何不熟悉的术语.

3. Bracket the main idea or thesis of the reading, and put an asterisk next to it.  
   用括号把所读材料的中心思想或论点括起来，并在旁边标一个星号.

4. Put down your highlighter. Make marginal notes or comments instead.  
   放下荧光笔，改为在页边做笔记或写评论.

5. Write questions in the margins, and then answer the questions in a reading journal or on a separate piece of paper.  
   把问题写在页边，然后在阅读日志里或另一张纸上回答这些问题.

6. Make outlines, flow charts, or diagrams that help you to map and to understand ideas visually.  
   制作提纲、流程图或图表，帮助自己以可视化的方式梳理和理解观点.

7. Read each paragraph carefully and then determine "what it says" and "what it does." Answer "what it says" in only one sentence.  
   仔细阅读每一段，然后判断它「说了什么」和「起了什么作用」；「说了什么」只用一句话回答.

8. Write a summary of an essay or chapter in your own words.  
   用自己的话写一篇文章或某一章的摘要.

9. Write your own exam questions based on the reading.  
   根据所读内容自己出考试题.

10. Teach what you have learned to someone else.  
    把自己学到的东西教给别人.

## Precis

(A concise rhetorical analysis of an article with 4 sentences.)

1st sentence: main idea or topic (if there is no main idea in some subtypes/baby modes, such as topic pattern or extended definition)  
第 1 句：中心思想或话题（若某些次类型／小类型，如话题型或扩展定义，没有中心思想，则写话题）.

2nd sentence: supporting ideas and ways or organization, using reporting verbs  
第 2 句：支撑性观点与组织方式，使用转述动词.

3rd sentence: purpose(s) of writing  
第 3 句：写作目的.

4th sentence: audience and language style.  
第 4 句：读者与语言风格.


> **例**：
>
> In the article, The McGraw Center for Teaching & Learning in Princeton University provides some active reading strategies for the undergraduates.
>
> This handout explains/interprets the strategies in/from 10 aspects, such as "Ask yourself pre-reading questions", "Identify and define any unfamiliar terms", "Bracket the main idea or thesis of the reading, and put an asterisk next to it", etc.
>
> The purpose of offering the strategies is to help the students "remember and analyze" what they read in their academic study.
>
> Since the target readers are the undergraduates, the language is simple-styled but formal and instructive by using the imperative mood.

## How to Summarize the Lecture

1st sentence: Identification about the source, the speaker, the topic and the main idea.  

the following sentence(s): Information about the big picture, key points and important details.

>**例**：
>
> In the lecture about product innovation, the professor states that successful innovators share some common qualities. He first defines "product innovation" as ways businesses get ideas for new products. He points out that in the past product innovation was mostly due to mistake or luck, but in the 21st century, companies spend a lot of money on innovation, and successful innovators have two common qualities.
>
> Then the professor claims that innovative companies usually have courage to take risks. A good example is Apple Computer, which invented iPod for people to be able to listen to music everywhere, and this made great profits for Apple.
>
> Next, the professor illustrates another common characteristic of innovative companies – thinking about their products in a new way. He gives the example of Starbucks that provides wireless Internet service to make the customers stay longer, which may probably help their profits increase.
>
> Finally, the professor restates that a totally new idea can change a business.

## Describing Graphs and Charts

**图表描述**

When describing a chart or a graph, a general description of the topic and the general trend(s) or change(s) is usually first given in the part of introduction. Descriptions of specific details or changes, including specific figures shown in the graph or chart should be given in the body to support the general trend(s).  
描述图表时，通常先在引言中概述主题以及总体趋势或变化，再在正文中描述具体细节或变化，引用图表中的具体数据来说明总体趋势。

**Introduction**  
**引言**

1. A brief introduction of the graph  
   简要介绍图表。
2. Description of the general trend  
   描述总体趋势。

**Body: Supporting details**  
**正文：支撑细节**

3. Description of <u>relevant</u> specific details  
   描述<u>相关</u>的具体细节。
4. Specific figures quoted when necessary  
   必要时引用具体数据。

**Conclusion**  
**结论**

5. Conclusion, summary or prediction at the end  
   在末尾给出结论、总结或预测。

- Ignore irrelevant numbers or figures.  
  略去无关的数字或数据。

There are some commonly used expressions in descriptions of graphs and charts.  
以下是描述图表时常用的一些表达。

**Expressions of introducing the topic:**  
**引出主题的常用表达：**

1. The chart below displays / shows / reveals / illustrates / describes / demonstrates / represents…  
   下图展示／表明／揭示／说明／描述／论证／呈现了……
2. The table offers us information about…  
   该表提供了关于……的信息。

**Expressions of describing general trend(s):**  
**描述总体趋势的常用表达：**

3. This pie diagram/bar chart/ line graph reveals that …  
   这张饼图／柱状图／折线图表明……
4. The figures show evidence that…  
   这些数据表明……
5. It can be seen from the table that…  
   从表中可以看出……
6. From the graph/diagram, it is noted/clear that…  
   从图表／示意图中可以看出……
7. As is shown/provided/released/can be seen in the chart, ….  
   如图表所示／所提供／所公布，或从图表中可以看出，……
8. As we can see clearly from the figures/chart, …  
   从这些数据／这张图表中可以清楚地看出，……
9. According to the figures shown in the table/graph/diagram/chart, we can see/discover that…  
   根据表格／曲线图／示意图／图表所列的数据，可以看出／发现……

**Expressions of describing specific details or changes: *(Give your attention to the uses of tense.)***  
**描述具体细节或变化的常用表达：*（注意时态的使用。）***

1. The number of … grew/rose/increased (decreased/declined/dropped/fell) from… to… in 2020.  
   2020 年，……的数量从……增加／上升（减少／下降）到……。
2. X increased (decreased) threefold/by 3 times/significantly/substantially/dramatically/marginally/ steadily/gradually/in big leaps/sharply/steeply to … in April.  
   4 月，X 增至原来的三倍（或降至原来的三分之一），也可用显著地／大幅地／剧烈地／小幅地／稳步地／逐渐地／跳跃式地／急剧地／陡峭地描述增减幅度或速度，最终达到……。
3. There was a big/huge/dramatic/massive/marked/sharp/sudden/gradual/steady/significant increase in …  
   ……出现了较大／巨大／剧烈／大规模／明显／急剧／突然／逐渐／稳步／显著的增长。
4. There appeared an even more tendency to rise/drop.  
   上升／下降的趋势更加明显。
5. X experienced / saw a significant rise/decline in the period from … to …  
   在……到……的这段时间里，X 显著上升／下降。
6. X reached a peak of / peaked at …/amounted to … in May/reached the bottom of … in December.  
   X 达到峰值……／在……处达到峰值／在 5 月达到……／在 12 月降至最低点……。
7. There was little change in …/no increase was shown in ….  
   ……变化很小／……未出现增长。
8. X experienced no change/remain static/stable/unchanged/the same in the first quarter of this year.  
   今年第一季度，X 没有变化／保持平稳／维持原状。
9. The difference between X and Y diminished after October.  
   10 月以后，X 和 Y 之间的差距缩小了。
10. A makes up/occupies 30% of the total output/income/expenditure/consumption.  
    A 占总产量／总收入／总支出／总消费量的 30%。
11. In 1990 there were only …, and in 1993 the number has reached …, but in 1995, it increased/dropped/fell/jumped to/by 13 per thousand.  
    1990 年只有……，1993 年这一数字达到了……。到 1995 年，它上升／骤升或下降到了千分之十三，或者升降了千分之十三（取决于用 to 还是 by）。
12. A is the largest one, with B comes next. Then there is C, … The smallest one appears to be D, which accounts for …  
    A 最大，B 次之，接着是 C，……。D 似乎最小，占……。
13. A is twice as large/high/much as B.  
    A 的大小／高度／数量是 B 的两倍。
14. A outnumbered B 3 to 1.  
    A 的数量超过 B，两者之比为 3∶1。
15. A remained ahead of B.  
    A 继续领先于 B。

**Expressions of drawing a conclusion:**  
**得出结论的常用表达：**

1. After considering the information in the graph, we might conclude that…  
   综合图中的信息，可以初步得出结论……
2. According to the figures shown in the table/graph/diagram/chart, we can conclude that…  
   根据表格／曲线图／示意图／图表中的数据，可以得出结论……
3. From/According to the statistics/study/ survey, it can be learned/estimated/predicted that …  
   根据统计数据／研究／调查，可以得知／估计／预测……
